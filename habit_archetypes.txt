HabiTribe: 12 Psychological Habit Archetypes
=============================================
This framework maps every user into one of 12 unique personality schemas during onboarding. The AI engine uses these to personalize quotes, UI elements, and "Restore Flow" friction advice. 
As users interact with the app, their archetype can dynamically evolve based on their behavior data.

1. The Warrior (The Disciplined Finisher)
   • Intent: Mastery through strict routine. They want to conquer challenges and see hard metrics.
   • Habits Strategy: High-intensity goals, rigid streaks, loves "Hard" difficulty habits.
   • Friction Advice Focus: "Don't burn out. Rest is part of the battle." 
   • Icon: 🗡️ or 🔥

2. The Architect (The Systems Builder)
   • Intent: Optimization. They care less about the raw streak and more about the "perfect system."
   • Habits Strategy: Multi-layered routines, habit-stacking, highly detailed notes.
   • Friction Advice Focus: "Don't let perfect be the enemy of good. Do 1% today."
   • Icon: 🏗️ or 📐

3. The Spark (The Burst Performer)
   • Intent: Quick wins. They get massive bursts of motivation but struggle with long-term consistency.
   • Habits Strategy: Short, 7-day sprints instead of 100-day grinds. High variety.
   • Friction Advice Focus: "Momentum is a wave. Learn to ride the small ripples when the big waves recede."
   • Icon: ⚡ or 🎆

4. The Sage (The Mindful Learner)
   • Intent: Deep growth and understanding. They want habits that expand their mind or soul.
   • Habits Strategy: Reading, meditation, journaling. Prefers quality of execution over daily streaks.
   • Friction Advice Focus: "Patience. A mountain is built one grain of sand at a time."
   • Icon: 🧘 or 👁️

5. The Guardian (The Protector of Streaks)
   • Intent: Consistency at all costs. They hate losing and are highly risk-averse to breaking the chain.
   • Habits Strategy: Daily check-ins, utilizes the "Streak Freeze" frequently.
   • Friction Advice Focus: "A skipped day does not erase a hundred days of progress."
   • Icon: 🛡️ or 🤝

6. The Explorer (The Novelty Seeker)
   • Intent: Variety. They get bored doing the exact same thing every day.
   • Habits Strategy: Rotating habits, trying completely new challenges every month.
   • Friction Advice Focus: "Boredom is just the brain asking for a deeper level of focus."
   • Icon: 🧭 or ⛵

7. The Caregiver (The Community Anchor)
   • Intent: Social accountability. They will show up for their Tribe even if they wouldn't show up for themselves.
   • Habits Strategy: Highly active in Tribes, motivated by team goals and sharing progress.
   • Friction Advice Focus: "You cannot pour from an empty cup. Take time for your own habits."
   • Icon: 💚 or 🕊️

8. The Alchemist (The Re-inventor)
   • Intent: Radical transformation. Usually joining the app after a major life event or "rock bottom" moment.
   • Habits Strategy: Drastic lifestyle changes, highly susceptible to the "Elastic Recovery" flow.
   • Friction Advice Focus: "Real magic takes time. Trust the slow process of transmutation."
   • Icon: 🧪 or 🔮

9. The Anchor (The Unshakable Base)
   • Intent: Stability and groundedness. They move at a slow, reliable, unemotional pace.
   • Habits Strategy: Easy/Medium habits, immune to motivation drops, rarely uses the app socially.
   • Friction Advice Focus: "You are the rock. But remember, even rocks can be polished to shine brighter."
   • Icon: ⚓ or 🏔️

10. The Visionary (The Big Picture Dreamer)
    • Intent: Reaching the ultimate end-goal. They focus heavily on the destination, neglecting the daily steps.
    • Habits Strategy: Vision boards, grand goals, needs constant reminders of "why" they are doing this.
    • Friction Advice Focus: "The dream is alive, but the path is built today. Focus on the next step."
    • Icon: 🔭 or 🌌

11. The Optimist (The Resilient Bouncer)
    • Intent: Positive momentum. They rarely feel bad about breaking a streak and happily just click "start over."
    • Habits Strategy: Flexible, joyful habits. Highly responsive to positive reinforcement in the UI.
    • Friction Advice Focus: "Your positivity is your superpower. Channel it into a 5-day streak this week."
    • Icon: ☀️ or 🌻

12. The Artisan (The Craft Purist)
    • Intent: Quality and beauty over quantity. They skip days if they can't do the habit perfectly.
    • Habits Strategy: Long sessions (e.g., 2 hours of guitar) instead of 10 minutes every day.
    • Friction Advice Focus: "A messy sketch is better than a blank canvas. Put something down today."
    • Icon: 🎨 or ⚒️

---
HOW TO USE IN ONBOARDING QUIZ:
Q1: "When you miss a habit, how do you feel?" -> Maps to Optimist / Guardian
Q2: "What motivates you most?" -> Maps to Caregiver / Architect / Warrior
Q3: "How do you prefer your timeline?" -> Maps to Spark / Sage / Anchor
Q4: "What's your primary goal?" -> Maps to Visionary / Explorer / Alchemist
Q5: "How does your routine look?" -> Maps to Artisan / Architect / Warrior
